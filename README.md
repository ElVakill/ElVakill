### About me

- **Engineering student**, member of the **ENSMA Racing Team** (Formula Student) in the **Vehicle Dynamics** division — I work on the steering model and the team's shared Python calculation library.
- **Creator of [Personae](https://personagame.fr)**, a free social deduction game for iOS and Android: friends answer questions about each other anonymously, and each player discovers how the group sees them.
- Interested in vehicle dynamics, simulation, and building products end to end — from the physics to the app store.
